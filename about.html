About Foodie Reviews

Our Story

Founded in 2025, Foodie Reviews was created by a group of food enthusiasts who wanted to share their culinary experiences with the world. We believe everyone deserves to find great food in their city.

Our Mission

To provide honest, unbiased restaurant reviews from real people. We're not paid by restaurants - our only loyalty is to good food!

Our Team

We're a team of students who are also passionate eaters dedicated to helping you discover your next favorite meal.
